How MarqueeFlow works

From owner registration to daily operations — a secure, approval-based onboarding flow.

Owner registers from APK

Marquee owner downloads the MarqueeFlow app and creates a business account with name, phone, and hall details.

Pending approval

New businesses start in pending status until the MarqueeFlow Super Admin team verifies and approves the marquee.

Super Admin approves

Once approved, the owner gets full access to subscriptions, packages, slots, bookings, and team management.

Set up packages & slots

Owner creates hall packages, manages calendar slots, and starts accepting bookings with payment tracking.

Invite Manager & Waiter Head

Managers and Waiter Heads cannot self-register publicly. They join only through owner invite by name, role, and phone.
