Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

MarqueeFlow ("we", "our", "us") operates the public website at marqueeflow.com, the business mobile application, and the internal Super Admin control panel. This policy explains how we collect and use information when you visit our website or use our services.

Information we collect

  • Contact details submitted through demo request forms (name, phone, business name, city, message).
  • Business account information provided by marquee owners and invited team members in the mobile app.
  • Operational data such as bookings, customers, payments, and packages entered by business users.
  • Technical logs required for security, authentication, and platform reliability.

How we use information

  • To provide booking, calendar, payment, package, and team management features.
  • To review new marquee registrations and manage platform subscriptions.
  • To respond to demo requests, support inquiries, and service communications.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and improve the MarqueeFlow platform.

Data separation

Super Admin accounts, business user accounts, and public website submissions are handled separately. Business owners and staff use the mobile app. Internal MarqueeFlow administrators use the Super Admin panel only.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. Information may be shared only when required by law, to protect platform security, or with service providers that help us operate MarqueeFlow under appropriate safeguards.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact us at support@marqueeflow.com or via WhatsApp from our contact section.
